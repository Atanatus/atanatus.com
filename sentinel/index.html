What it does

  1. Binance Convert: realized USDC profit only (not owner deposits).
  2. Pack to BCH on Binance.
  3. Withdraw to an exclusive Sentinel cashaddr that starts at 0.
  4. When a lot is allowed: swap the BCH needed for ATAN, then LP ATAN+BCH at ~50/50 value after the swap (not 50% of coin count, not a naive half-and-half of the BCH bag).
  5. Normal BCH leg ≤ 10% of ATAN-pooled BCH. Hard cap 30%. Rest stays in Sentinel treasury.
  6. If swap works and LP cannot finish: stop. No second buy. Residual ATAN is pending LP, not a hold.

What it does not do

  • Buy ATAN to hold, support price, or buy back.
  • Use founder / reserve keys or wallets.
  • Spend owner BCH sitting on an old Cauldron login.
  • Chase price. Abnormal book → WAIT.
