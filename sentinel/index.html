How Sentinel decides on Cauldron

BCH that leaves Binance sits on the cashaddr until size and pool state allow an action that does not shove the glass people already trade.

  1. Withdraw txid is listed here. No txid → not Sentinel.
  2. Below about 0.01 BCH on the cashaddr: wait. The exchange minimum alone is not a buy and not an LP.
  3. A single lot is capped at about 30% of BCH already in ATAN pools on Cauldron (read the public swap page / pool API).
  4. HOLD = buy ATAN at the screen price and keep it in the Sentinel wallet. Reserve.
  5. LP = open a new micro-pool at the current ATAN/BCH ratio. Not a round token count chosen in advance.
  6. If the lot would move the glass too hard, it is split or skipped.
  7. Founder LP and Sentinel LP are different wallets. Sentinel does not use the founder passkey.

Waiting is an action. Spending BCH only because it arrived is not.
